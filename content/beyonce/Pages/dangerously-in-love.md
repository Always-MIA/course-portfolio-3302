# Dangerously in Love

![[Dangerously-in-love.jpg]]

**Release Date:** June 24, 2003

## Overview

*Dangerously in Love* is Beyoncé's debut solo studio album. Released after Destiny's Child went on hiatus, it established her as a successful solo artist through a blend of R&B, pop, soul, and hip-hop influences.

## Background

The album was recorded between 2002 and 2003 with producers including Rich Harrison, Scott Storch, and Missy Elliott. Beyoncé used the project to showcase her vocal range while exploring more personal themes than her previous work with Destiny's Child.

## Notable Songs

- Crazy in Love (feat. Jay-Z)
- Baby Boy (feat. Sean Paul)
- Naughty Girl
- Me, Myself and I

## Reception

The album debuted at number one on the Billboard 200 and won five Grammy Awards.

## Legacy

The album launched Beyoncé's solo career and remains one of the most influential R&B albums of the early 2000s.
