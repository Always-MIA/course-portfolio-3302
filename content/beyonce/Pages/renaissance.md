# Renaissance

![[renaissance.jpeg]]

**Release Date:** July 29, 2022

## Overview

*Renaissance* celebrates dance music, house, disco, and ballroom culture while serving as the first act of Beyoncé's planned trilogy.

## Background

Created during the COVID-19 pandemic, the album was inspired by dance floors and Black queer musical history.

## Notable Songs

- Break My Soul
- Cuff It
- Alien Superstar
- Virgo's Groove

## Reception

The album won multiple Grammy Awards and became one of the most critically acclaimed albums of 2022.

## Legacy

*Renaissance* revived mainstream interest in dance and house music while celebrating LGBTQ+ communities and Black musical traditions.
