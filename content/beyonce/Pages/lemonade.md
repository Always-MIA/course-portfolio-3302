# Lemonade

![[lemonade.jpg]]

**Release Date:** April 23, 2016

## Overview

*Lemonade* is a visual album exploring themes of identity, love, betrayal, race, resilience, and empowerment.

## Background

Accompanied by a feature-length film, the album blends R&B, rock, blues, country, gospel, and hip-hop influences.

## Notable Songs

- Formation
- Hold Up
- Sorry
- Freedom

## Reception

The album received universal acclaim and numerous Grammy nominations.

## Legacy

Many critics consider *Lemonade* one of the greatest albums of the twenty-first century.
