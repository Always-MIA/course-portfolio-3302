# 4

![[4.jpg]]

**Release Date:** June 24, 2011

## Overview

*4* showcases Beyoncé's appreciation for classic R&B, soul, funk, and gospel influences.

## Background

After taking greater creative control over her music, Beyoncé created an album inspired by artists from the 1970s and 1980s.

## Notable Songs

- Love on Top
- Countdown
- Best Thing I Never Had
- Run the World (Girls)

## Reception

Critics praised the album's vocal performances and mature songwriting.

## Legacy

The album has become a fan favorite and demonstrated Beyoncé's artistic independence.
