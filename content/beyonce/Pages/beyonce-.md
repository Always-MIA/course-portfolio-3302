# Beyoncé

![[beyonce.png]]

**Release Date:** December 13, 2013

## Overview

Released without prior announcement, *Beyoncé* became one of the most influential surprise album releases in music history.

## Background

Every song was accompanied by a music video, creating one of the first major visual albums.

## Notable Songs

- Drunk in Love
- XO
- Pretty Hurts
- Partition

## Reception

The album debuted at number one and received widespread critical acclaim.

## Legacy

The surprise release transformed how artists approach album marketing.
