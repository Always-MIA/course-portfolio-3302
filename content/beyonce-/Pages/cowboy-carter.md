# Cowboy Carter

![[cowboy-carter.jpg]]

**Release Date:** March 29, 2024

## Overview

*Cowboy Carter* is Beyoncé's eighth solo studio album and the second act of her trilogy. The album blends country, Americana, folk, rock, pop, and R&B while celebrating the often-overlooked contributions of Black artists to country music.

## Background

The project features collaborations with country musicians and reimagines traditional country sounds through Beyoncé's artistic perspective.

## Notable Songs

- Texas Hold 'Em
- 16 Carriages
- II Most Wanted (feat. Miley Cyrus)
- Jolene

## Reception

The album debuted at number one in multiple countries and received widespread critical acclaim for its ambitious concept and genre exploration.

## Legacy

*Cowboy Carter* sparked conversations about diversity within country music and reinforced Beyoncé's reputation for challenging genre boundaries.
