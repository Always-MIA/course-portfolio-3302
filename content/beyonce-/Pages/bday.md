# B'Day

![[bday.jpg]]

**Release Date:** September 4, 2006

## Overview

Released in celebration of Beyoncé's twenty-fifth birthday, *B'Day* features energetic production inspired by funk, soul, and R&B.

## Background

The album was recorded in only a few weeks and emphasized live instrumentation and upbeat performances.

## Notable Songs

- Déjà Vu
- Irreplaceable
- Ring the Alarm
- Upgrade U

## Reception

The album debuted at number one on the Billboard 200 and became a commercial success worldwide.

## Legacy

*Irreplaceable* became one of Beyoncé's signature songs, while *B'Day* highlighted her growth as a performer.
