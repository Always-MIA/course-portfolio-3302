# I Am... Sasha Fierce

![[sasha-fierce.jpg]]

**Release Date:** November 18, 2008

## Overview

The double album explores Beyoncé's personal side and her confident stage persona, Sasha Fierce.

## Background

The album was divided into two discs to contrast emotional ballads with energetic pop songs.

## Notable Songs

- Single Ladies (Put a Ring on It)
- Halo
- If I Were a Boy
- Diva

## Reception

The album won six Grammy Awards and became one of the biggest releases of 2008.

## Legacy

"Single Ladies" became one of the defining songs of the decade.
