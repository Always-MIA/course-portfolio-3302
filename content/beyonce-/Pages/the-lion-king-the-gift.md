# The Lion King: The Gift

![[lion-king-gift.jpg]]

**Release Date:** July 19, 2019

## Overview

Created alongside Disney's *The Lion King*, this album celebrates African music, storytelling, and culture through collaborations with artists from around the world.

## Background

Beyoncé curated the project to highlight African musicians and celebrate the film's themes.

## Notable Songs

- Spirit
- Brown Skin Girl
- Bigger
- Already

## Reception

Critics praised its diverse collaborations and cultural significance.

## Legacy

The album introduced many global listeners to African artists and musical styles.
