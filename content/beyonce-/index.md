# Beyoncé

## Overview

Beyoncé is an American singer, songwriter, actress, and businesswoman. After rising to fame as a member of Destiny's Child, she launched a solo career in 2003 that has become one of the most successful in music history. Her work blends pop, R&B, soul, hip-hop, country, and dance music while often exploring themes of empowerment, identity, love, and culture.

## Related Categories

### Renaissance

*Renaissance* introduced listeners to Beyoncé's love of house and dance music while celebrating Black and LGBTQ+ dance culture. The album serves as the first act in a planned trilogy that continued with *Cowboy Carter*.
