# Kiss Land

![[kiss-land.jpg]]

**Release Date:** September 10, 2013

## Overview

*Kiss Land* is The Weeknd's debut studio album. The album expands on the dark, atmospheric sound of his earlier mixtapes while exploring themes of fame, isolation, and life on tour.

## Background

After gaining widespread attention through his trilogy of mixtapes, The Weeknd worked with longtime collaborators including Illangelo and Doc McKinney to create a cinematic debut album.

## Notable Songs

- Kiss Land
- Belong to the World
- Live For (feat. Drake)
- Pretty

## Reception

The album received generally positive reviews from critics, who praised its ambitious production and storytelling.

## Legacy

Although initially viewed as a departure from his mixtapes, *Kiss Land* has since become a fan favorite and is regarded as an important step in The Weeknd's artistic development.
