# Dawn FM

![[dawn.jpg]]

**Release Date:** January 7, 2022

## Overview

*Dawn FM* is a concept album presented as a fictional radio broadcast that explores themes of mortality, reflection, and the afterlife through synth-pop and electronic music.

## Background

Narrated in part by actor Jim Carrey, the album continues the story introduced on *After Hours* while drawing inspiration from 1980s pop and dance music.

## Notable Songs

- Take My Breath
- Sacrifice
- Out of Time
- Less Than Zero

## Reception

Critics praised the album's concept, production, and seamless transitions between tracks.

## Legacy

*Dawn FM* further demonstrated The Weeknd's ability to create immersive concept albums and expanded his reputation as an innovative pop artist.
