# Starboy

![[starboy.jpg]]

**Release Date:** November 25, 2016

## Overview

*Starboy* explores themes of fame, luxury, and personal transformation while incorporating pop, R&B, electronic, and hip-hop influences.

## Background

The album features several collaborations with Daft Punk and reflects a brighter, more polished sound than The Weeknd's earlier work.

## Notable Songs

- Starboy
- I Feel It Coming
- Reminder
- Die For You

## Reception

The album debuted at number one in multiple countries and was praised for its production and hit singles.

## Legacy

*Die For You* became a global hit years after its release, especially following the remix featuring [[ariana-grande/index|Ariana Grande]].
