# Beauty Behind the Madness

![[beauty.jpg]]

**Release Date:** August 28, 2015

## Overview

*Beauty Behind the Madness* marked The Weeknd's commercial breakthrough, blending alternative R&B with mainstream pop and electronic influences.

## Background

The album features collaborations with producers such as Max Martin and includes contributions from Lana Del Rey, Labrinth, and Ed Sheeran.

## Notable Songs

- Can't Feel My Face
- The Hills
- Earned It
- In the Night

## Reception

The album debuted at number one on the Billboard 200 and received widespread critical acclaim, earning two Grammy Awards.

## Legacy

The success of *Beauty Behind the Madness* established The Weeknd as one of the biggest pop artists in the world.
