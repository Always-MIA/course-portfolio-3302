# Hurry Up Tomorrow

![[the-weeknd-hurry-up-tomorrow.jpg]]

**Release Date:** January 31, 2025

## Overview

*Hurry Up Tomorrow* is The Weeknd's sixth studio album and concludes the trilogy that began with *After Hours* and *Dawn FM*. The album blends pop, R&B, electronic, and cinematic influences while exploring themes of identity, redemption, and closure.

## Background

The album features collaborations with several artists and producers and serves as the final chapter in the narrative arc surrounding The Weeknd persona.

## Notable Songs

- Timeless
- São Paulo
- Open Hearts
- Cry for Me

## Reception

The album debuted at number one in multiple countries and received positive reviews for its ambitious production, storytelling, and emotional depth.

## Legacy

As the conclusion to one of modern pop music's most acclaimed album trilogies, *Hurry Up Tomorrow* represents a significant milestone in The Weeknd's career and artistic evolution.
