# After Hours

![[after-hours.jpg]]

**Release Date:** March 20, 2020

## Overview

*After Hours* combines synth-pop, R&B, and new wave influences while telling a cinematic story of heartbreak, loneliness, and redemption.

## Background

Inspired by 1980s music and psychological thrillers, the album features a recurring red-suited character throughout its visuals and performances.

## Notable Songs

- Blinding Lights
- Heartless
- Save Your Tears
- In Your Eyes

## Reception

The album was both a commercial and critical success, with *Blinding Lights* becoming one of the most successful songs in Billboard history.

## Legacy

*After Hours* helped define pop music in the early 2020s and inspired a wave of retro-inspired production across the industry.
