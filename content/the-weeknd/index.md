# The Weeknd

## Overview

The Weeknd is the stage name of Canadian singer, songwriter, and producer Abel Tesfaye. His music blends R&B, pop, electronic, and alternative influences, earning him worldwide recognition for his cinematic storytelling and distinctive vocal style.

## Related Categories

### Ariana Grande

The Weeknd and [[ariana-grande/index|Ariana Grande]] have collaborated on several successful songs, including **Love Me Harder**, **Off the Table**, **Save Your Tears (Remix)**, and **Die for You (Remix)**.

Fans have also noticed that some of their songs transition seamlessly into one another, leading to discussions about whether these connections were intentionally planned. Their frequent collaborations and similar musical influences create a strong relationship between their discographies.
