# About Me Page

## About Me

Hi! I'm **Mia Singleton**, a 22-year-old originally from **Louisiana** who now calls **Texas** home. I'm currently pursuing a bachelor's degree in **Computing Applications** while working as a **flight attendant**, which has given me the opportunity to travel and experience different places and cultures.

Music has always been one of my biggest passions. I've been playing the **violin** since I was nine years old, and I also love to sing. Although my busy schedule doesn't leave me as much time for my hobbies as it used to, I still enjoy music whenever I get the chance.

One of my favorite interests is exploring pop music and learning about the artists behind it. That's what inspired this knowledge base, which highlights the solo discographies of **Ariana Grande, Beyoncé, Charli XCX, The Weeknd, and Zara Larsson**. Organizing information about artists and their albums has allowed me to combine my love of music with the information organization concepts I've learned throughout this course.

---

## About This Knowledge Base

This project is organized using a **hierarchical structure**. The homepage serves as the main entry point, where users can navigate to each artist's page. Each artist page links to individual album pages, and every album page contains links to the previous album, the next album, and back to the artist's index. This interconnected structure makes it easy to browse the collection while demonstrating the principles of digital information organization.
