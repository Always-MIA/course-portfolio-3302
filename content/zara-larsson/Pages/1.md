# 1

![[1.png]]

**Release Date:** October 1, 2014

## Overview

*1* is Zara Larsson's debut studio album, released exclusively in Sweden. It combines pop, dance-pop, and R&B influences while introducing Larsson's powerful vocals and songwriting.

## Background

Following her victory on the Swedish television competition *Talang* in 2008, Zara Larsson signed a recording contract and released several successful EPs before debuting with *1*.

## Notable Songs

- Uncover
- Carry You Home
- Rooftop
- Bad Boys

## Reception

The album reached number one on the Swedish Albums Chart and established Larsson as one of Sweden's most promising young pop artists.

## Legacy

*1* launched Zara Larsson's recording career and paved the way for her international success.
