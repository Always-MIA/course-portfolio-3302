# Midnight Sun

![[midnight-sun.jpg]]

**Release Date:** September 26, 2025

## Overview

*Midnight Sun* is Zara Larsson's fifth studio album. Inspired by the long summer days of Sweden, the album blends dance-pop, electropop, and electronic music while exploring themes of ambition, identity, confidence, friendship, and personal growth.

## Background

Released through Larsson's own label, Sommer House, in partnership with Epic Records, *Midnight Sun* was executive-produced by MNEK and serves as the follow-up to *Venus*. The album reflects Larsson's artistic maturity and draws inspiration from Swedish summers and club culture.

## Notable Songs

- Midnight Sun
- Pretty Ugly
- Crush
- Blue Moon

## Reception

The album received positive reviews from critics, who praised its cohesive dance-pop sound, confident songwriting, and polished production. Many reviewers considered it one of Larsson's strongest projects to date. 

## Legacy

*Midnight Sun* further established Zara Larsson as one of Sweden's leading contemporary pop artists. Its celebration of Scandinavian culture and modern dance-pop showcased her continued artistic evolution while expanding her global audience.
