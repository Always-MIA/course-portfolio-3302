# So Good

![[so-good.jpg]]

**Release Date:** March 17, 2017

## Overview

*So Good* is Zara Larsson's second international studio album, featuring a blend of dance-pop, R&B, and electronic music that helped establish her as a global pop star.

## Background

The album includes collaborations with artists such as Ty Dolla $ign and MNEK while building upon the international success of "Lush Life."

## Notable Songs

- Lush Life
- Never Forget You
- Ain't My Fault
- Symphony (with Clean Bandit)

## Reception

The album received positive reviews and achieved commercial success across Europe and several other international markets.

## Legacy

*So Good* introduced Zara Larsson to a worldwide audience and remains her breakthrough international album.
