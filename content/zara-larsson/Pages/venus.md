# Venus

![[venus.jpg]]

**Release Date:** February 9, 2024

## Overview

*Venus* explores themes of empowerment, self-confidence, and relationships while blending dance-pop, electronic, and contemporary pop influences.

## Background

The album marked a new creative era for Larsson and features collaborations with several acclaimed producers and songwriters.

## Notable Songs

- Can't Tame Her
- On My Love
- You Love Who You Love
- End of Time

## Reception

The album received generally positive reviews, with critics highlighting its energetic production and confident songwriting.

## Legacy

*Venus* represented a new chapter in Zara Larsson's career and set the stage for her next artistic evolution.
