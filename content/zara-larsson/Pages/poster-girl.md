# Poster Girl

![[poster-girl.jpg]]

**Release Date:** March 5, 2021

## Overview

*Poster Girl* is Zara Larsson's third studio album and features polished dance-pop production alongside themes of love, confidence, and personal growth.

## Background

Created during the COVID-19 pandemic, the album reflects Larsson's evolving artistic style while incorporating contemporary pop production.

## Notable Songs

- Love Me Land
- Talk About Love
- Ruin My Life
- Wow

## Reception

Critics praised the album's catchy production and Larsson's vocal performances.

## Legacy

*Poster Girl* demonstrated Zara Larsson's continued growth as an international pop artist.
