# Zara Larsson

## Overview

Zara Larsson is a Swedish singer and songwriter known for her powerful vocals and upbeat pop music. Since winning the Swedish television competition *Talang* as a child, she has become an internationally recognized artist with multiple chart-topping singles and collaborations.

### Swedish Pop

Zara Larsson is part of a long tradition of successful Swedish pop artists. Her music combines dance-pop, electropop, and contemporary pop influences while continuing Sweden's reputation as one of the world's leading producers of popular music.
