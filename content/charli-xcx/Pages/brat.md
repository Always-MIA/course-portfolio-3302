# Brat

![[brat.png]]

**Release Date:** June 7, 2024

## Overview

*Brat* is Charli XCX's sixth studio album and celebrates club music, confidence, vulnerability, and internet culture. The album became one of the defining pop releases of 2024.

## Background

Inspired by rave, electronic, and dance music, *Brat* showcases Charli's signature hyperpop sound while exploring themes of fame, friendship, and self-reflection.

## Notable Songs

- Von Dutch
- 360
- Apple
- Sympathy Is a Knife

## Reception

*Brat* received universal critical acclaim and appeared on numerous year-end "Best Albums" lists, with critics praising its production, songwriting, and cultural impact.

## Legacy

The album inspired the viral "Brat Summer" trend and further cemented Charli XCX as one of pop music's most influential and innovative artists.
