# Charli

![[charli.jpg]]

**Release Date:** September 13, 2019

## Overview

*Charli* features numerous collaborations and blends mainstream pop with hyperpop and electronic influences.

## Background

The album followed several critically acclaimed mixtapes and represented Charli's vision of collaborative, forward-thinking pop music.

## Notable Songs

- 1999
- Gone
- Cross You Out
- White Mercedes

## Reception

The album received widespread critical acclaim for its innovative production and ambitious collaborations.

## Legacy

Many critics consider *Charli* one of the defining pop albums of 2019 and a milestone in the evolution of hyperpop.
