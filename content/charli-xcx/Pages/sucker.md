# Sucker

![[sucker.png]]

**Release Date:** December 15, 2014

## Overview

*Sucker* shifted Charli XCX toward a more punk-inspired pop sound while maintaining her catchy songwriting and energetic style.

## Background

Following the success of "Boom Clap," Charli embraced guitar-driven production influenced by punk rock and new wave music.

## Notable Songs

- Boom Clap
- Break the Rules
- Doing It
- Famous

## Reception

The album received generally positive reviews and expanded Charli XCX's international audience.

## Legacy

*Sucker* established Charli XCX as a successful pop artist while showcasing her versatility as a songwriter.
