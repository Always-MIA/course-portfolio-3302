# Crash

![[crash.png]]

**Release Date:** March 18, 2022

## Overview

*Crash* embraces mainstream pop influences while maintaining Charli XCX's distinctive songwriting and creative vision.

## Background

Released as her final album under Atlantic Records, *Crash* explores dance-pop, synth-pop, and 1980s-inspired production.

## Notable Songs

- Good Ones
- Beg for You
- Baby
- Used to Know Me

## Reception

The album debuted at number one in the United Kingdom and received positive reviews from critics.

## Legacy

*Crash* became Charli XCX's highest-charting album in several countries and demonstrated her ability to balance commercial appeal with artistic identity.
