# True Romance

![[true-romance.jpg]]

**Release Date:** April 12, 2013

## Overview

*True Romance* is Charli XCX's debut studio album. Blending synth-pop, electropop, and dark wave influences, the album introduced her distinctive songwriting style and experimental approach to pop music.

## Background

Following several mixtapes, Charli XCX worked with producers including Ariel Rechtshaid and Patrik Berger to create an album inspired by internet culture, romance, and alternative pop music.

## Notable Songs

- Nuclear Seasons
- You (Ha Ha Ha)
- What I Like
- Grins

## Reception

Critics praised the album's originality and production, with many recognizing Charli XCX as an emerging voice in modern pop.

## Legacy

Although not a major commercial success, *True Romance* became a cult favorite and laid the foundation for Charli XCX's future experimental sound.
