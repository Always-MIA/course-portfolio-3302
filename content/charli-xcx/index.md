# Charli XCX

## Overview

Charli XCX is an English singer, songwriter, and producer known for redefining modern pop music. Throughout her career she has combined mainstream pop with experimental electronic production, helping popularize the hyperpop movement while remaining one of pop's most innovative artists.

## Related Categories

### Hyperpop

Charli XCX is widely credited as one of the artists who helped bring hyperpop into the mainstream. Her collaborations with producers such as A. G. Cook and Sophie influenced both her own music and many newer pop artists.
